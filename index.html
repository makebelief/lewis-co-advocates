About Lewis & Co
Advocates

Dedicated counsel.
Practical results.

Lewis & Co Advocates is a Nairobi-based law firm with a strong focus on dispute resolution, corporate and commercial law, and representation in a wide range of court proceedings.

Founded by Advocate Lewis Gicheha Karanja, the practice provides considered guidance to clients facing complex commercial, employment, property, criminal and family-law issues.

Strong advocacy begins with careful preparation and a clear strategy.
